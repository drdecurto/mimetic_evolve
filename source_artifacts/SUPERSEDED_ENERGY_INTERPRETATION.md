# Superseded exploratory energy interpretation

The archives `mimetic_analysis_v2.zip` and `mimetic_analysis_v3.zip` are retained verbatim for provenance. Their exploratory energy-growth tables evaluate an unnormalised Euclidean symmetric part and are not the quantities reported in the analysis. The order-eight value near `844.9` is additionally inadmissible as an energy-norm statement because the associated stored weight is indefinite.

The authoritative analysis is `scripts/analyze_downstream_v3.py`, with outputs under `derived/downstream_analysis_v3/`. It uses the generalized relative $Q$-energy rate, finite-time $Q$-semigroup amplification, and excludes indefinite weights from energy claims.
